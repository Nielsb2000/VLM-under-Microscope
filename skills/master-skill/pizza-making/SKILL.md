### Step 3: Shape the Pizza
1. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C)
2. Punch down the dough to release air
3. Transfer to a floured surface
4. Stretch and shape into a 12-inch circle
5. Place on a pizza pan or baking sheet
6. Brush edges with olive oil

### Step 4: Add Toppings
1. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border for crust
2. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese as the base layer
3. Add desired toppings in a single layer
4. Sprinkle remaining cheese on top
5. Season with dried oregano and basil if desired

### Step 5: Bake
1. Place in preheated oven
2. Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is melted and bubbly
3. Remove from oven when edges are slightly charred

### Step 6: Serve
1. Let pizza cool for 2-3 minutes
2. Slice using a pizza cutter
3. Serve hot and enjoy!

## Tips

- For faster preparation, use store-bought pizza dough
- Don't overload with toppings - keep it to 2-3 toppings for best results
- Use a pizza stone for crispier crust (preheat in oven for 30 minutes)
- Add toppings after the first 5 minutes of baking for a crispier crust
- Fresh basil can be added after baking for extra flavor
