# Traditional Pizza Making

## Overview

This skill explains how to make a delicious homemade pizza from scratch, including all necessary ingredients, preparation steps, and cooking instructions.

## Ingredients (1 Person / 1 Pizza)

### For the Dough
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 0.25 teaspoon salt
- 0.125 teaspoon sugar
- 0.5 tablespoon olive oil
- 0.25 teaspoon instant yeast
- 0.3 cup warm water

### For the Toppings
- 0.25 cup pizza sauce or tomato sauce
- 0.5 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- Toppings of choice (pepperoni, mushrooms, peppers, onions, olives, etc.)
- 0.5 tablespoon olive oil (for brushing crust)
- Dried oregano and basil (optional)

## Scaling Up

To make pizza for multiple people, simply multiply all ingredient quantities by the number of servings:
- **2 pizzas:** Multiply all ingredients by 2
- **4 pizzas:** Multiply all ingredients by 4
- **Example:** For 2 pizzas, use 2 cups flour, 0.5 teaspoon salt, 1 teaspoon yeast, etc.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Dough
1. Mix warm water, yeast, and sugar in a bowl
2. Let sit for 5 minutes until foamy
3. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt
4. Pour yeast mixture into flour
5. Add olive oil and mix until a dough forms
6. Knead for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic

### Step 2: First Rise
1. Place dough in a greased bowl
2. Cover with a damp cloth
3. Let rise for 1-2 hours until doubled in size
4. The dough is ready when poking it leaves an indentation

### Step 3: Shape the Pizza
1. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C)
2. Punch down the dough
3. On a floured surface, roll or stretch dough into a 12-inch circle
4. Transfer to a pizza pan or baking sheet
5. Create a slightly raised edge for the crust

### Step 4: Add Toppings
1. Brush edges with olive oil
2. Spread pizza sauce evenly, leaving the edge clear
3. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese
4. Add your chosen toppings
5. Sprinkle with oregano and basil if desired

### Step 5: Bake
1. Bake for 12-15 minutes
2. Pizza is done when the crust is golden and cheese is bubbly
3. Let cool for 2-3 minutes before slicing

## Tips for Success

- Use warm (not hot) water for yeast activation
- Let dough rise in a warm, draft-free area
- Don't overload with toppings or the crust will be soggy
- For a crispier crust, pre-bake for 5 minutes before adding toppings
- Use a pizza stone for best results (preheat it with the oven)

## Common Variations

### Margherita Pizza
- San Marzano tomatoes
- Fresh mozzarella
- Fresh basil leaves
- Extra virgin olive oil

### Pepperoni Pizza
- Pizza sauce
- Mozzarella cheese
- Pepperoni slices

### Vegetarian Pizza
- Pizza sauce
- Mozzarella cheese
- Bell peppers, mushrooms, onions, olives, tomatoes

## Troubleshooting

- **Dough too sticky:** Add flour 1 tablespoon at a time
- **Dough too dry:** Add water 1 teaspoon at a time
- **Crust not browning:** Increase oven temperature or bake longer
- **Soggy center:** Use less sauce or pre-bake crust
