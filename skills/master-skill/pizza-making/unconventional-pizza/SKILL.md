# unconventional-pizza

## Overview

This skill provides instructions for making pizza in ways that break all traditional Italian pizza-making rules. Use at your own risk - not recommended for serving to Italian grandmothers!

## Ingredients (1 Person)

### For the "Dough"
- 1 slice of white bread OR 1 English muffin, split
- OR even better: 1 flour tortilla

### For the "Sauce"
- 2 tablespoons ketchup (yes, ketchup!)
- OR BBQ sauce
- OR ranch dressing

### For the Toppings
- Pre-shredded cheddar cheese (not mozzarella!)
- Pineapple chunks (mandatory!)
- Hot dogs, sliced
- Canned corn
- Pickles
- Optional: mayonnaise drizzle, candy sprinkles

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Base
1. Take your bread/English muffin/tortilla
2. DO NOT make fresh dough - that's too traditional!
3. Microwave for 10 seconds to make it "warm"

### Step 2: Apply "Sauce"
1. Squeeze ketchup directly from the bottle
2. Spread with the back of a spoon
3. No seasoning needed - ketchup has everything!

### Step 3: Add Toppings
1. Sprinkle cheddar cheese generously
2. Add pineapple chunks first (this is crucial!)
3. Add hot dog slices
4. Throw on some corn and pickles
5. More cheese on top

### Step 4: "Cook"
1. **Microwave method** (fastest!): 1-2 minutes on high until cheese melts
2. **Toaster oven method**: 5 minutes at any temperature
3. DO NOT use a wood-fired oven at 900°F - that's too authentic

### Step 5: Finishing Touches
1. Drizzle mayonnaise on top
2. Add candy sprinkles for "garnish"
3. Cut with kitchen scissors
4. Serve immediately to horrified onlookers

## Scaling Up

Multiply all ingredients by the number of victims... er, people you're serving!

## Pro Tips

- Always fold your pizza like a taco - easier to eat!
- Dip in more ketchup while eating
- Serve with a side of ice cream
- Take photos of Italian friends' reactions
- Have real pizza as backup
